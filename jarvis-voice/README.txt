1. Right-click hey-jarvis.zip, choose "Extract All", open the folder, double-click "Install Hey Jarvis".
2. When it says Done, just say "Hey Jarvis" and the Jarvis page pops up. It starts by itself at every login.
3. A blue dot near the clock means it is listening; right-click it to Pause or Quit.
4. "Stop Hey Jarvis" stops it for now; "Uninstall Hey Jarvis" removes it completely.
5. Private: all listening happens on this laptop; no audio is saved or sent anywhere.
