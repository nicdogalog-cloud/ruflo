2. What Crease Cam collects

Videos and photos

Clips you record or pick in Crease Cam are saved in your own phone's storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. When you record, the app removes location information (GPS) from the clip, so a clip does not show where it was filmed.

Your age answer and settings

When you first open the app it asks your age, so it can show a parent or guardian step for younger players. This answer, and your app settings and session notes, are stored on your phone only.

Subscriptions

If you buy Pro, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) takes the payment. They tell the app whether your subscription is active. We do not receive your name, card details or address from them. Apple and Google handle your payment data under their own privacy policies.

When you contact us

If you email us, we get your email address and whatever you write, so we can reply.

This website

The website uses no cookies, ads or analytics. Like any website, our hosting provider ([hosting provider, e.g. Cloudflare]) briefly processes your IP address and basic browser details to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or anything to build a profile of you. There are no third-party ad or tracking tools in the app. If we ever add privacy-friendly, cookie-free statistics or crash reports, we will update this policy first.

3. Why we use your data (our lawful bases)

  • To provide Pro you paid for (performing our contract with you): checking your subscription status.
  • To answer you and keep things secure (our legitimate interests): replying to emails, protecting the website.
  • To meet the law (legal obligation): keeping business and tax records.
  • Consent, where we ask for it: for example a parent's consent for a player under 13.

4. Children and young players

Crease Cam is for cricketers of all ages. We follow the UK Children's Code: privacy settings are high by default, we collect as little as possible and we never nudge young players to share more. Under UK law, players under 13 need a parent or guardian's consent to use an online service like ours, so the app shows a parent step for them. In some other countries the age is higher (up to 16); the same parent step applies there.

For young players: your videos stay on your phone and we can't see them. Always ask before you film someone else, and ask a grown-up if you're not sure.

7. Deleting your data

Delete any clip in the app or in your phone's gallery, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. You can also use "Delete my data" in the app's settings, or email [contact email] and we will delete anything we hold about you. To stop a subscription, cancel it in your Apple or Google account.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, delete or hand over the personal data we hold about you, or to stop or limit how we use it. Parents can make these requests for a young child. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first, and you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Keeping data safe

Because your clips never leave your phone, they are protected by your phone's own lock. We keep the little data we hold secure, and if something ever goes wrong we will follow our breach plan, tell the ICO when the law requires it and tell you if you are at risk.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Crease Cam uses data, for example by adding cloud uploads, we will update this policy and tell you in the app before the change happens.
