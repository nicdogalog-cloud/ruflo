My tripod keeps getting knocked. What helps?

Move it back towards 2 metres behind the stumps so the bowler has more room, and spread the legs fully. Check the phone is tight in the clamp before you start.

My video looks dark or blurry.

Wipe the lens first. Film with the light behind the phone, not shining into it. Indoors, pick a spot under a light and away from bright windows.

Where does the phone go?

Always the same spot: on a tripod 1 to 2 metres behind the bowler's stumps, at head height. Use zoom 1x for bowling and 3x for batting.

Will the ball hit my phone?

Behind the bowler's stumps is the safest place in a net, because batters rarely hit the ball back that hard and straight. Never put the phone on the pitch, and make sure the bowler has room to run past.

My phone says storage is full.

Crease Cam saves clips to your phone, so delete clips you no longer need from your camera roll, then film again.
