Most net footage is filmed from the wrong place. Too far away, too low, or at an angle that hides the thing you wanted to see.

A coach knows where to stand to see your front foot land or your head fall over. A phone propped against a kit bag doesn't.

A net has no safe space beside the batter, so Crease Cam uses one spot that works in every net: a tripod just behind the bowler's stumps. Set it up once and every session is worth watching back.

The camera spot

One spot. Behind the bowler.

Phone on a tripod, 1 to 2 metres behind the bowler's stumps. Same spot for batting and bowling. Just change the zoom.

Set up

  1. Put the tripod 1 to 2 m behind the bowler's stumps, in line with them.
  2. Raise the phone to about head height, landscape, so it sees over the bowler.
  3. Check the bowler has room to run past.
  4. Turn on slow-mo (120 or 240 fps).

BowlingZoom 1x

Wide, so you and the whole pitch fit in.

  • Last strides into the crease
  • Front foot landing and no-balls
  • Arm and release point
  • Line, and where it pitches
  • Follow-through

BattingZoom 3x

Tight on the batter, over the bowler's shoulder.

  • Head still or falling over
  • Stance and trigger movement
  • Where the front foot goes
  • Straight bat or across the line
  • Balance at the end of the shot

One honest limit: this angle is front-on, so it can't show a true side view of your stride or back-lift.

How it works

Set up in a minute.
Then just play.

  1. Pick what you're working on

    Batting or bowling. The app sets the zoom for you: 3x for batting, 1x for bowling.

  2. Set up the tripod

    1 to 2 metres behind the bowler's stumps, phone at head height. A small phone tripod is all you need.

  3. Record your session

    Film as you normally would. No sensors and no account to set up.

  4. Watch it back properly

    Replay in slow motion and frame by frame to see exactly what happened on each ball.

After every session

One score. One thing to fix.

When you finish, Crease Cam sums the session up on a single screen. No wall of stats, just the one change most worth making next time.

Privacy and safety

Your clips never leave your phone.

Clips stay on the phone

Everything you film is saved in your own phone storage. We don't upload it, store it or see it.

No ads or tracking

There are no third-party ads or tracking tools in the app, and no ads shown to young players.

A parent step for younger players

The app asks your age when you first open it. Younger players get a parent or guardian step before anything else.

Film safely, with permission

Keep the tripod behind the bowler's stumps, never on the pitch. Ask before filming anyone else, and for juniors ask a parent too.

FAQ

Questions players and parents ask

Something else? Message us on Instagram.

When can I download Crease Cam?

Crease Cam is coming soon to iPhone and Android. The App Store and Google Play links will appear here at launch. Follow @creasecam07 on Instagram, or DM us "waitlist" to hear the day it's out.

Do I need any extra kit?

Just a small phone tripod (about £15) that raises your phone to head height. That and the phone you already have.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere. They stay on your phone. Crease Cam doesn't upload your clips, so they're as private as the rest of your camera roll.

Is it OK for under-18s?

Yes. Crease Cam is for players of all ages. Younger players go through a parent or guardian step when they first open the app, and there are no ads or trackers.

Does it track the ball or measure speed?

No. Crease Cam does the step before analysis: getting the camera in the right place. Good clips from the right angle are useful on their own or alongside other cricket apps.

What do I get with Pro?

Side-by-side compare of two clips, drawing lines and angles on a paused frame, and saved sessions with notes. Pro is £3.99 a month or £39.99 a year at launch.

Which countries is it available in?

Anywhere the App Store and Google Play allow it. Prices are set in GBP and your store shows them in your local currency.
