How it works

Three steps at the nets

Filmed your net session and all you got was the back of the bowler's head? A badly placed phone gives you a clip you can't learn from. Crease Cam fixes that.

  1. 1

    Pick what you're working on

    Batting or bowling, front view or side view. Each one has its own camera spot and guide card.

  2. 2

    Place your phone with the guide card

    The card shows where the phone goes, how high, and which way it faces. Keep it behind or beside the net, never on the pitch.

  3. 3

    Record and replay

    Film your session, then watch it back in slow motion and frame by frame. No sensors, no extra kit, just the phone you already have.

In the app, each card also shows "what your phone sees" from that spot, with setup steps for distance, height and which way to face.

Privacy and safety

Your videos never leave your phone.

Crease Cam is built for players of every age, including juniors. Here's what that means for players and parents.

Clips stay on the phone

Everything you film is saved in your own phone storage. We don't upload it, store it or see it.

No ads for kids, no tracking

No third-party ads or tracking tools in the app, and no ads shown to young players.

A parent step for younger players

The app asks your age when you first open it. Younger players get a parent or guardian step before anything else.

Film safely and with consent

Keep the phone behind or beside the net, never on the pitch. Ask before filming anyone else, and for juniors ask a parent too. Follow your club's safeguarding rules.

FAQ

Questions players and parents ask

When can I download Crease Cam?

Crease Cam is coming soon to iPhone and Android. The App Store and Google Play links will appear here at launch. Follow @creasecam07 on Instagram or DM us "waitlist" to hear the day it's out.

Do I need any extra kit?

No. You need the phone you already have. A cheap clamp or small flexible tripod helps hold it on the net or a fence pole.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere. They stay on your phone. Crease Cam doesn't upload your clips, so they're as private as the rest of your camera roll.

Is it OK for under-18s?

Yes. Crease Cam is for players of all ages. Younger players go through a parent or guardian step when they first open the app, and there are no ads or trackers.

Does it track the ball or measure speed?

No. Crease Cam does the step before analysis: getting the camera in the right place. Good clips from the right angle work on their own or alongside other cricket apps.

What do I get with Pro?

Side-by-side compare of two clips, drawing lines and angles on a paused frame, and saved sessions with notes. Pro is £3.99 a month or £39.99 a year (launch pricing).

Which countries is it available in?

Crease Cam will be available wherever the App Store and Google Play allow it. Prices are set in GBP and your store shows them in local currency.
